## 1. CONTRIBUTION STATEMENT (final, incorporating reviewer corrections A1/A3)

We contribute a **fair-comparison instrument for catastrophic forgetting in PEFT**
— a method-neutral, adaptation-weighted update-magnitude axis (F_Δ, CLoRA's own
Eq-3 diagnostic), per-method LR sweeps, and eval-matched calibration for
data-aware initializations — that decomposes any adapter's apparent retention win
into a magnitude effect, an LR-tuning artifact, and a calibration artifact.
Applied across **1,035 adapters — 4 model×task settings under 6 training recipes,
2 architectures, 7 assessed methods, 3–5 seeds** — retention tracks F_Δ at pooled
r = −0.847 (flat-then-falling with per-family knees), and a commonality-split
nested regression shows magnitude's unique variance share is **18× that of
update-shape geometry** (+0.296 vs +0.016, order-independent; §19.1 + 06 §5).
Update magnitude is the **first-order predictor and control variable** of
forgetting — NOT framed as the proximal mechanism: our own mediation analysis
(05) shows behavioral drift (KL) is the proximal channel in 4/6 families, with
magnitude the upstream knob. Geometry is real, seed-stable, portable (it recurs
at 284B), and second-order for forgetting. LR is a proxy (relation holds inside
every fixed-LR stratum). An interventional arm (E1, Llama-CS) upgrades the
central claim from correlation to control: rescaling a trained adapter moves it
along the curve; a random direction at matched magnitude buys no adaptation.
CLoRA's published numbers are corroborating external evidence throughout.
