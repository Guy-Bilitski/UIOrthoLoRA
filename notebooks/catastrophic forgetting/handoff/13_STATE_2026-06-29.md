## 1. Project & central claim
Controlled study of **what governs catastrophic forgetting in PEFT**. We fine-tune **Llama-2-7B**
(replicating on **Qwen2.5-7B**) with 7 LoRA-family adapters and measure **adaptation vs retention**.
**CENTRAL CLAIM = THE LAW:** *retention is governed by the weight-update magnitude ‖ΔW‖_F, not by the
adapter method* — adapters fall on one retention-vs-‖ΔW‖ curve, so the simplest magnitude control
(LoRA + weight decay) matches elaborate structured inits. We do NOT headline "LoRA is the best method".
(The old UIOrthoLoRA goal is DEAD; that instrument is gone from the active campaign.)
