## Additional analysis actually completed

1. Per-seed reaggregation uses the same 27 summaries and 1,296 module rows; raw CSVs are unchanged. The main table has 15 A/B/C task/condition rows, not a selected subset of seeds. Generated appendix tables expose all 27 records.
2. The Haar-projector identity is an analytical result: for a fixed scaler independent of a uniformly random rank-k projector in dimension d, expected penalty is k(d-k)/((d-1)(d+2)) times its squared centered norm. Penalty-only gradient flow conserves the mean. This motivates CENTER and RANDPROJ, but does not establish their joint training behavior.
3. Initialization values are computed from the documented recipe: leading core I, uniform ambient scalers s0 and tail h0 I. Initial norm is s0² sqrt(k+r h0²). Initial leading fraction exceeds 99.5% for the listed settings, but the relative initial magnitude is not recoverable without the original pretrained matrix norms. Do not turn a large fraction into a large absolute or functional effect.
4. Logged training-call durations sum to approximately 14.9755 hours over these 27 records. That is neither total project compute nor verified GPU-hours: setup/post-training diagnostics, failed/tuning runs and per-run hardware attribution are missing. Peak values are allocated CUDA bytes after a pre-training reset, not reserved/process/setup memory.
5. CPU tests cover the new projector expectation, mean conservation, initialization, identity and chordal identities, sample SD calculations and cost rows, in addition to the original algebra tests. CPU random-matrix checks are not PEFT model experiments.

## Recommendations not adopted literally

- **Moving GLUE entirely to the appendix or replacing Avg6 with Avg5:** conflicts with the author's current request and settled MRPC metadata. The table stays in the main paper, separated by source and stripped of causal/ranking implications.
- **Replacing imported LoRA numbers with canonical published values and declaring the issue fixed:** original protocols differ in rank, intermediate MNLI adaptation, aggregation and tuning. That would create another unmatched comparison, not establish fairness. Preserve the historical numbers and run P5.
- **Declaring frame invariance or identical penalties from a common scalar nullspace:** incorrect. Projector weights differ away from the nullspace. The new expectation identity has an independence condition; an optimized scaler is generally dependent on its training projector.
- **Declaring identity-core collapse, statistical isotropy, or no benefit from SVD proven:** not supported by three aggregate fractions or reviewer synthetic examples. Store and measure the corresponding alternatives prospectively.
- **Treating penalty-only flow as proof that joint AdamW training shrinks the scalar mean:** unsupported. The mean is conserved under the penalty-only flow; task gradients/weight decay change the situation.
- **Using norm/gap as an unconditional drift bound, or zero cross mixing as top-k stability:** not valid without separation hypotheses. Tail singular-value crossings remain possible.
- **Computing a pooled nine-sign p-value or inferring significance from hundreds of dependent modules:** independence is not established. Use paired seed reports and appropriately qualified intervals.
- **Changing the small reported RTE standard deviation on granularity grounds:** a mean/SD across seed counts can vary by fractions of a single-run accuracy increment. Recover source artifacts; do not invent a numeric correction.
- **Treating PDF-extracted missing retention cells as actual missing entries:** inspection of the original table showed the numbers were present. Its exclusion is for scientific/provenance reasons, not a fabricated missing-cell problem.
